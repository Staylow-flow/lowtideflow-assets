    The Screen Factor

    Every ink color in your design requires its own physical screen setup before a single shirt hits the press. More colors mean more screens, more labor, and more setup time. Keeping your palette tight—especially on smaller runs—is one of the fastest ways to protect your per-unit cost.

    Power of Volume

    Fifty pieces is our starting line because screen printing is a volume game. At one hundred and two hundred fifty pieces, automated press efficiencies kick in hard: setup is amortized across more units, ink and handling costs drop, and your per-shirt price falls dramatically. The calculator above reflects those real production breakpoints.

    Quality Blanks

    We print on heavyweight, retail-grade garments—think Gildan Hammer and Hanes W120—because durability and hand-feel matter more than shaving pennies off cheap, thin cotton. Your brand lives on the body. We build orders to outlast the event, the season, and the wash cycle.

    Artwork Guidelines & Policies

    Free Setup & Prep: If you have your logo, graphics, or branding finalized and ready to print, we provide free professional type-setting, sizing optimization, and digital mockups to make sure it prints flawlessly.

    Custom Concept Design: Need killer, ground-up creative work or complex illustration? That is our specialty, but we don’t work on spec. True custom apparel design requires a upfront design deposit, locked-in milestone approvals, and a strict “pay to play” structure. Let us know in your project brief if you need a creative consult.
