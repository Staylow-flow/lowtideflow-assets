The Screen Factor

Every ink color in your design requires its own physical screen setup before a single shirt hits the press. More colors mean more screens, more labor, and more setup time. Keeping your palette tight—especially on smaller runs—is one of the fastest ways to protect your per-unit cost.

The Power of Volume

Fifty pieces is our starting line because screen printing is a volume game. At one hundred and two hundred fifty pieces, automated press efficiencies kick in hard: setup is amortized across more units, ink and handling costs drop, and your per-shirt price falls dramatically. Premium Blanks

We print on heavyweight, retail-grade garments—think Gildan Hammer and Hanes Beefy-T—because durability and hand-feel matter more than shaving pennies off cheap, thin cotton. Your brand lives on the body. We build orders to outlast the event, the season, and the wash cycle.
